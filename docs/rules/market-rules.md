# Market Rules

**Effective 10/29/2026 · Version 1.0**

Monoglarket is a play-money prediction market. These rules regarding conduct on the site sit alongside the [Terms of Service](/terms). 
<!-- If the two conflict, the Terms of Service apply. -->

**MONOGLARKET DOES NOT ENDORSE, SUPPORT, OR CONDONE ANY REAL-WORLD ACTIONS TAKEN BY INDIVIDUALS IN CONNECTION WITH MARKETS.**

## 1. Who Can Propose and Resolve a Market

- Anyone may suggest a market. Only moderators and administrators can create and publish markets.
- Send suggestions to an administrator on Discord (`@aidenpearce3066`) or by email to `markets@monoglarket.jmulligan191.com`.
- Any user that isn't an administrator that is able to resolve or manage a market is prohibited from doing so in any market in which they hold any trades, positions, or shares.

## 2. Prohibited Markets

Markets must not violate any of the rules below.

### 2.1 Real Money and Physical Prizes

Real money, physical prizes, or anything with real-world value may **never** be used as a stake, entry fee, payout, or any other function on the site. See the [Terms of Service](/terms) for the full policy.

### 2.2 Market Integrity

We allow markets about the decisions or actions of individuals. However, we may forfeit winnings from any participant who could influence the outcome of such a market, for example by controlling or directly affecting the outcome, or by using non-public information about it.
- Forfeiture may apply to that market's winnings. In serious cases, administrators may take further action.

### 2.3 Privacy of Individuals

Markets about a private individual's health, romantic relationships, personal finances, or private life are prohibited unless that individual has given explicit written consent.

### 2.4 Institutional Policy and Governance

Monoglarket operates within the influence of RIT and the SSE, meaning markets must also comply with their policies and the rules of any relevant affiliated organization. In particular:
- Markets about politics or political events are prohibited at every level, including internal club or campus governance, and local, state, national, and international politics.
- Markets that involve, encourage, or facilitate activity that is illegal or prohibited by RIT are banned.

### 2.5 Community Standards

Markets with content that is offensive, hateful, deceptive, harassing, discriminatory, or that can potentially encourage unethical behavior are prohibited.

### 2.6 Discretion

Administrators and moderators may decline, reject, or remove any proposed or active market, even if these rules don't explicitly cover it.

## 3. Resolution and Voiding

- A moderator resolves a market by posting evidence visible to all participants.
- Automated markets draw their outcomes only from sources reviewed and configured by site maintainers. Only an administrator can finalize an automated resolution. Automated markets are labeled as such on the site.
- We may void, cancel, or re-resolve any market that was resolved in error, relies on unverifiable information, or breaks these rules (see [Terms of Service, Section 4.2](/terms)).
- All trades in a voided market will be reversed or voided depending on the circumstances and will be decided by the moderator or administrator handling the market.

## 4. Trading and Market Lifecycle

- Trades are final once confirmed, except to correct clear technical errors or when a moderator intervenes under the Terms of Service.
- Trading locks automatically at the lock time, the close time, or upon resolution, whichever comes first.
- Participants may report any market or its result to moderators. While they investigate, moderators may temporarily hide the market or restrict trading on it. 

## 5. Enforcement and Appeals

- Violations may result in a warning, forfeiture of winnings, removal of a market, or restriction of an account, depending on severity.
- Participants may appeal a moderator decision by contacting an administrator within 2 days. An administrator who was not involved in the original decision (if available) will review the appeal.

## 6. Changes to These Rules

We may update these rules at any time. Changes apply to markets proposed after the new version takes effect. Active markets are governed by the version in effect when they were proposed, unless an administrator decides otherwise for any reason including safety or integrity. Changes to these rules will be announced to users via the website or other official communication channels. It is the responsibility of users to stay informed about updates. By continuing to use the site, you agree to the latest rules.
