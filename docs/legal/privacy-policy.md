### Information from other sources

- **Google.** If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address, and name, and a profile picture if you have one. We request only the basic profile information needed for authentication.
- **Discord (optional).** If you connect Discord, we request the `identify`, `email`, and `connections` scopes, plus `role_connections.write` so we can show roles. We store your Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar, your Discord email and whether it is verified, and your connected accounts. We also store your OAuth access and refresh tokens, encrypted at rest. You can unlink Discord on your account page.
- **GitHub (optional).** If you connect GitHub, we request no scopes. We use it only to confirm you control the GitHub account listed on your SSE profile. We store your GitHub login and numeric ID and the time we checked. We do not keep your GitHub access token.
- **LinkedIn (optional).** If you connect LinkedIn, we request the `openid` scope and store your LinkedIn identifier and the profile link you gave us.
- **SSE website roster.** The Service mirrors public records from the SSE website, including member, officer, mentor, membership, event, and attendance records. These may include your name, email address, graduation information, major, description, co-op summary, profile picture, and any linked accounts that appear on your SSE profile. This information is copied from SSE's systems, and you can ask us to correct it (Section 8).
- **RIT API.** We use RIT's public term data to convert graduation terms to term codes. We do not receive personal information from RIT through this API.
- **Your device.** When you visit the Service, your browser sends standard information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. Our hosting server and reverse proxy may log this as part of routine operation.

We collect aggregate and anonymized information about how members use the Service, for example which markets are most popular and which features are used most. This helps us improve the Service. We do not use this information to identify you.
We also collect information regarding trading activity, including the number of trades, the number of shares bought and sold, and the total amount spent and earned. We are required to do this to comply with the Illinois Gambling Act and to maintain the integrity of the fake-money markets. This information is not shared with third parties, except as required by law or as described in this policy.
We do not use  advertising trackers, or third-party tracking scripts in the Service.

## 2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- sign you in and keep your session secure;
- check that you are eligible to use the Service (for example, that you are 18 or older and use a permitted email domain);
- run markets, record trades, keep the ledger accurate, and pay allowances and awards;
- show your name, username, profile, pronouns (if public), and activity to other members as the Service is designed to;
- manage SSE membership, officer, mentor, event, and attendance records and the play-money rewards tied to them;
- send sign-in codes and links, and notifications you have chosen to receive;
- moderate content, handle reports, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service;
- back up, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service; and
- comply with law and respond to valid legal requests.

## 3. Who we share information with

**Service providers.** We share information only with providers who help us run the Service, under obligations to keep it confidential and use it only for our purposes. ## 4. Cookies and browser storage

We use only cookies that the Service needs:

| Cookie | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| Session cookie | Keeps you signed in. It is HTTP-only and holds a random token. We store only a hash of the token. | 30 days |
| Pending email cookie | Remembers the email address you entered during sign-in, so you can enter your code | 15 minutes |
| OAuth state cookies (Google, GitHub, LinkedIn, Discord) | Protect the connection steps against forgery | 10 minutes |

Because these cookies are needed to run the Service, they do not require consent under the rules we follow, and you cannot turn them off without losing access. Our service worker stores files so the Service can load and receive push notifications. Your browser's settings control whether these are kept.

## 6. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), store passwords as salted scrypt hashes, store sign-in codes and session tokens as hashes, encrypt stored Discord tokens, and limit access to the database to authorized club staff. Nightly backups are stored on the club's server. No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us at [contact email] right away.

## 7. Where information is stored

The Service runs on servers in the United States, operated by SSE and RIT. If you use the Service from outside the United States, you consent to your information being transferred to and processed in the United States.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can:

- **See and edit** most of your information on your account page, including name, username, pronouns, location, and profile picture.
- **Unlink** Discord, GitHub, LinkedIn, or Google on the account page.
- **Turn off push notifications** on the account page or in your browser or phone settings.
- **Take a break** from trading, which pauses your ability to trade until the date you choose. Only an administrator can end it early, in the circumstances described in the Terms of Service.
- **Remove your birthday** from your profile. Birthdays are set once and can be removed, but cannot be changed or re-added.
- **Ask for access, correction, or deletion** of your personal information by writing to [privacy contact email]. We will respond within [30] days and may ask you to verify your identity first. Some information must be kept for the club's records, for example ledger entries and audit history. In that case we will tell you what we are keeping and why, and we will remove or anonymize the rest.
- **Opt out of optional emails** that are not sign-in codes. Sign-in messages are needed to use the Service.

The Service does not currently offer a self-service button to delete an account; please email us to make a deletion request. [Counsel and SSE to confirm whether to add one.]
